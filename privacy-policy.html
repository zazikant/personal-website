Privacy Policy

effective · 15 september 2026 · updated · 15 september 2026

Shashikant Zarekar ("DoqueRAG", "we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our website, products, and services (collectively, the "Services"). By using DoqueRAG, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

01 — Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of data:

a. Contact Information

Name, email address, phone number, company, and plan preference that you voluntarily provide through the contact form on our website.

b. Document Data

DoqueRAG uses a BYOK (Bring Your Own Keys) model. You provide your own Supabase, Pinecone, and LLM API keys. Your document content never touches DoqueRAG's servers. Documents are processed entirely within your own infrastructure.

c. Usage Information

IP address (for country code detection only), browser type, timestamps, and interaction logs with the contact form.

d. Cookies

We use cookies to remember your cookie consent preference. We do not use tracking cookies, ad cookies, or analytics cookies.

02 — BYOK and Data Ownership

DoqueRAG is a thin orchestration layer. All data — documents, embeddings, queries, and answers — lives in your own infrastructure:

  • Supabase — your document metadata table
  • Pinecone — your vector index
  • LLM API — your own API key (recommended: GLM 5.1)

We do not have access to your Supabase, Pinecone, or LLM accounts. We cannot read your documents, queries, or answers.

03 — Contact Form Data

When you submit the contact form, we collect:

  • Name, email, phone number, company, plan preference, and message
  • This data is sent via SMTP email to shashikant.zarekar@gemengserv.com
  • We use this data solely to respond to your enquiry and set up your instance
  • Contact form data is retained in the email inbox and deleted upon request

We do not sell, rent, or share contact form data with third parties.

04 — Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the requested services (setup assistance)
  • Fulfill legitimate business or legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes or enforce our agreements

When no longer required, data is securely deleted. Your document data in your own infrastructure is governed by your own retention policies — we have no access to it.

05 — Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Request a copy of your stored data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

You can exercise these rights by emailing zazikant@gmail.com. We respond in accordance with applicable data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, DPDP Act India).

06 — Security Measures

We use administrative and technical security measures, including:

  • SMTP email transmission via encrypted connection (SSL/TLS port 465)
  • No document content stored on our servers (BYOK architecture)
  • Rate limiting on the contact form API (5 requests per minute)
  • Input validation and sanitization on all form fields

No system is completely secure, but the BYOK model ensures your sensitive data never leaves your control.

07 — Children's Privacy

DoqueRAG is not directed to or intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

08 — Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated via the website.
